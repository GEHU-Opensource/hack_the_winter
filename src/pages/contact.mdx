## How to Reach

### Haldwani

Haldwani is the nearest major city to Bhimtal, located approximately 25 km away. It is well-connected by road, rail and air. 
Busses & taxis to Bhimtal are readily available from Haldwani.

Haldwani City [Location Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PtnyqEpj2csszXRA6)

##### To Haldwani By Air

Pantnagar Airport is the nearest airport, located approximately 30km from Haldwani. 
Regular flights connect Pantnagar to major cities like Delhi and Dehradun.

Pantnagar Airport [Location Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/wxsPTrHTdFzZEuXf7)

##### To Haldwani By Train

Haldwani Railway Station is well-connected to major cities in India.
Regular trains run between Haldwani and cities like Delhi, Lucknow, and Kathgodam.

Haldwani Railway Station [Location Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QQJ7xcjncy1csbGp8)\
*(Haldwani bus station is in walking distance from the railway station)*

Kathgodam Railway Station [Location Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Cqd1q6ECrtm58tJH6)

##### To Haldwani By Bus

Haldwani has a well-connected bus network with regular services to and from nearby cities and towns.

Haldwani Bus Station [Location Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MdzhruyVYBdqbnQW6)

### From Haldwani to Bhimtal

Bhimtal is approximately 25 km from Haldwani. You can reach Bhimtal by bus, taxi, or private vehicle.
