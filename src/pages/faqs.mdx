# FAQs

## Problem Statement for Online Rounds

Here are **8 themes**, you can choose any, and design your own problem statement. Just ensure you submit at Unstop before **31st Dec** over Unstop.

**How to submit:** Open our hackathon → go to Round 1 → click Submit → paste your GitHub repo link (as asked in the submission field).

---

## Theme Selection

Themes are fixed for **Round 1** and the same theme you choose over Round 1, you will just enhance that same project over **Round 2**. All students will need to attend both rounds for cumulative scores.

---

## About the Actual Hackathon

For the actual hackathon, the problem statements will come from **NITI Aayog**, same as SIH. These will be released at least a few days prior.

---

## ID Cards for Shortlisted Teams

**Q: How can shortlisted teams get their ID cards?**

Teams who successfully clear **Round 1** and **Round 2** will be shortlisted for the final hackathon. Once shortlisted, you can generate your official ID card from **[id.gehubhimtal.in](https://id.gehubhimtal.in)**.
